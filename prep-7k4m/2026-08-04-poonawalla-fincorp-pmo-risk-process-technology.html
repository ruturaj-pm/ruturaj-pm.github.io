Poonawalla Fincorp: PMO, Risk Process and Technology

Face-to-face round prep4 August 2026Lending risk technology

Role summary

  • Role: PMO for the Risk Process and Technology function.
  • Why the role exists: the company runs many risk-tech programmes in parallel, under a clear discipline mandate. A PMO keeps them on track.
  • What to expect: the first-round themes asked in more depth, plus culture and fit questions.
  • Fit hook: Poonawalla Fincorp loan repayments can be paid through Bharat Connect. "I worked on the rails your EMI collections ride on."

90-second pitch

"I'm a payments product manager with 8+ years across banking and payments infrastructure.

I spent three years at Kotak in payment-gateway operations. Merchant settlements, reconciliation, fee and MDR analytics. That is where I learned controls and process discipline.

Then I spent over three years at NPCI Bharat Billpay. I led the biller analytics platform. Then, as product manager, I launched the National Pension System on Bharat Connect. That meant coordinating three central recordkeeping agencies, the trustee bank and the regulator. That work was programme management as well as product: dependencies, steering cadence, UAT gates, go-live.

At NeoSOFT I built a greenfield reconciliation platform for a digital insurer, and I'm building compliance tooling now.

PMO in your risk-tech context is the muscle I've used at national scale. I want to apply it inside one BFSI franchise."

Two core answers

Why move on from NeoSOFT so soon?

"NeoSOFT is a services firm. I've shipped an insurer reconciliation build and compliance tools there. Now I want to build depth inside one BFSI franchise, and this role is exactly that."

Why PMO when you are a PM?

"Both roles deliver outcomes through others. A large part of my NPCI product work was programme orchestration across banks, recordkeeping agencies and a regulator. PMO in risk-tech uses that skill where the stakes are controls and compliance. That is where this company's transformation lives."
