The short answer

Earning is almost the same

Both give 5 points per Rs 150, and each point is worth Rs 1 on SmartBuy flights and hotels. That is 3.33% back. Hotels (10X) and flights (5X) on SmartBuy are the same on both.

Infinia has higher limits

Infinia can earn 15,000 SmartBuy bonus points a month, DBMC 10,000. Vouchers are 5X on Infinia and 3X on DBMC. These limits only matter if you book a lot of travel.

Getting and keeping it

Infinia is invite only, and from 1 Apr 2026 you must spend Rs 18 lakh a year on it or keep Rs 50 lakh with HDFC. DBMC is open to apply (net income above Rs 2.5 lakh a month) and has no rule to keep it.

Extras differ

Infinia: unlimited golf, Priority Pass, ITC stay 3 pay 2, no late fees. DBMC: 10,000 bonus points each quarter you spend Rs 4 lakh, and Club Marriott, Amazon Prime and Swiggy One after Rs 1.5 lakh in 90 days.

Getting the most from Diners Black Metal

Most of these also work on Infinia, which has higher limits. SmartBuy rates are from the September 2026 offer, and HDFC can change them each month.

01Book travel on SmartBuy, around the limit

  • Hotels earn 10X (about 33% back) and flights 5X (about 16.7%).
  • The DBMC bonus limit is 10,000 points a calendar month. You reach it at about Rs 33,300 of hotels or Rs 75,000 of flights.
  • For a big trip, book flights in one month and hotels in the next, so both earn the full bonus.
  • Compare the airline or hotel's own price before you book.

02Fill the voucher limit with shops you already use

  • SmartBuy vouchers earn 3X on DBMC, on up to Rs 45,000 a month. Rs 10,000 of vouchers earns 990 points, 660 more than paying the shop directly.
  • No-fee vouchers: Swiggy Money and Zepto (Rs 10,000 a month each). BigBasket, Blinkit, Zomato and Amazon Fresh (Rs 2,000 each). Uber, Tata CLiQ, Nykaa, Decathlon and Croma. BookMyShow and District on SmartBuy's Woohoo store.
  • Buy the month's Gyftr vouchers in one cart. BookMyShow and District check out separately on Woohoo, and Insta Deal vouchers need their own cart. Points count per full Rs 150 of each purchase.
  • Pay Amazon directly. Its Shopping Voucher carries a 3.5% fee plus GST, and SmartBuy's pages disagree on the fee for the Amazon Pay gift card.
  • Flipkart vouchers carry a 2.5% fee plus GST. The Flipkart Insta Deal (5% off) still comes out about Rs 200 ahead of paying Flipkart directly on each Rs 10,000.
  • Pick the Insta Deal discount for PVR INOX (15% off), Domino's (17.5%) and Netmeds (15%). It beats the points there.
  • In a month with a big SmartBuy hotel booking, skip vouchers. The hotel alone can use the whole 10,000 bonus.

03Swiggy: compare with the Swiggy HDFC card

  • The older Swiggy HDFC card pays 10% back on Swiggy food, Instamart and Dineout, up to Rs 1,500 a statement, on orders of Rs 249 or more. That beats a Swiggy voucher on DBMC, which gives about 9.9% in points.
  • HDFC is moving these cards to new versions until March 2027. The free Swiggy Ornge card pays 5%. With that card, the DBMC voucher wins if you use points for travel.

04Aim for the quarterly bonus

  • DBMC adds 10,000 points in each calendar quarter (Jan to Mar, Apr to Jun, Jul to Sep, Oct to Dec) where you spend Rs 4 lakh.
  • If your usual spend falls short, bunch big payments into one quarter: a yearly insurance premium, a trip, a large purchase, family bookings you pay for.
  • HDFC's exclusion list for this bonus names only EMI and wallet loads.

05Time the welcome spend

  • Spend Rs 1.5 lakh in the first 90 days to get Club Marriott, Amazon Prime and Swiggy One. The card terms say a year for each. One line on HDFC's page says 3 months for Swiggy One.
  • A yearly insurance premium due soon after the card arrives can help. The terms do not exclude insurance, but they do not define net retail spend either.

06Insurance premiums

  • Premiums earn normal points, capped at 5,000 points a month (about Rs 1.5 lakh of premium). Spread big premiums across months.
  • Check whether your insurer charges more for monthly mode. If it does, stay on yearly premiums. Points are the same per rupee either way.
  • Check that the insurer takes Diners and what it charges. Tata AIA's page names Diners only for card auto-debit, and its own FAQ leaves Diners off that list. The fee is up to 1.5% plus GST. You still come out ahead if the points go to travel.

07Weekend restaurants

  • Double points (10 per Rs 150, about 6.7% back) at standalone restaurants on Saturday and Sunday.
  • Pay with the card at the restaurant. Paying from a wallet balance, such as District or Swiggy Money, loses the double points. HDFC does not say whether paying by card inside an app counts.

08Other SmartBuy shops

  • 10X: Pharmeasy, IGP, FernsNPetals, Jockey and Adani One duty free (until March 2027).
  • 5X: Myntra (better than the Myntra voucher), MakeMyTrip holiday packages and buses.

09Spend points where they are worth most

  • SmartBuy flights and hotels: Rs 1 a point, up to 70% of a booking. Products give Rs 0.50 and cash on the bill Rs 0.30.
  • For international business class, move points 1:1 to Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer on HDFC's redemption form (no fee on Diners Black, 5 to 7 working days). A travel blog (PointsMax, June 2026) puts Delhi to Singapore business at 46,000 miles against a fare of Rs 85,000 to 1,00,000. That is about Rs 1.7 to 2.1 a point after the Rs 3,000 to 8,000 in taxes the blog mentions.
  • For economy, miles work out at about Rs 0.6 to 1.1 a point, about the same as SmartBuy or worse.
  • Check the ratio on SmartBuy after you log in with the card. The public list shows lower rates.
  • IndiGo is outside HDFC's transfer list. Add your BluChip number to IndiGo flights booked on SmartBuy to earn BluChips as well as card points.

10Avoid the traps

  • No points on rent, fuel, wallet loads, government payments or EMI. Turning a purchase into EMI later takes its points back.
  • 1% fee on rent. If your utility bills pass Rs 50,000 in a calendar month, 1% is charged on that month's whole utility total, capped at Rs 4,999.
  • Late fees apply on DBMC (up to Rs 1,300 plus GST). Set autopay for the full amount.
  • Points expire after 3 years, or all at once if the card is unused for a year.
  • Some sites and apps take only Visa and Mastercard, and HDFC gives no figure for how widely Diners is accepted in India. Keep one Visa or Mastercard as a backup.
  • Watch the Rs 8 lakh fee waiver line. Missing it costs Rs 11,800.
How the calculator works, and what it leaves out
  • Base points: 5 for every full Rs 150 of eligible spend, on both cards.
  • SmartBuy bonus is on top of base points: hotels 45 extra points per Rs 150, flights 20, vouchers 20 on Infinia and 10 on DBMC. This matches HDFC's own example, where Rs 50,000 of hotels earns 14,985 bonus points on Infinia.
  • Bonus limits per month: Infinia 15,000, DBMC 10,000, with vouchers limited to 3,000 inside that.
  • DBMC weekend dining earns 5 extra points per Rs 150. DBMC adds 10,000 points in any quarter where your spend (excluding rent, fuel and wallet loads) reaches Rs 4 lakh.
  • Fee: joining fee in the first year. In a later year the fee is charged unless your total spend reaches the waiver line. GST of 18% is added. Infinia adds 12,500 points whenever its fee is paid.
  • Not counted: the value of memberships, lounges, golf, insurance and hotel offers, and the monthly limits on grocery, utility and telecom points (2,000 each on both cards).
  • SmartBuy rates are from the offer that runs 1 to 30 Sep 2026. HDFC can change them each month.

Travel cards · beyond Infinia

What compares with Diners Black?

Keep Diners Club Black Metal on one side. Pick a rival on the other. Compare the travel you actually use, rather than treating every reward point as a rupee.

Same conversation, different strengths

ATLAS: the travel-earning rival

Earn accelerated miles on direct airline and hotel bookings, not only a bank portal. Transfer value depends on the partner and redemption. Lounge visits are tier-limited.

Emeralde Metal and AURUM: lounge rivals

Emeralde Private Metal matches unlimited domestic and international access. AURUM combines unlimited international access with 16 domestic visits a year and 1.99% forex.

Amex Travel: a milestone alternative

The current milestones are Rs 1.9, 4 and 7 lakh. The Rs 10,000 Taj voucher is now at Rs 7 lakh, not Rs 4 lakh. Eight domestic lounge visits do not match Diners' lounge coverage.

Regalia Gold: a cheaper step below

Rs 2,500 annual fee, spend-linked domestic lounges and flight vouchers. Useful at a lower budget, but not an equal-tier replacement for Diners Black Metal.

Before calling one the winner

Reward points are not the same currency

Diners' up-to-Rs 1 flight/hotel value is not cash back. ATLAS miles and Amex points have partner-specific values. Do not compare their earn rates as guaranteed percentages.

Foreign spend has a real cost

Forex markup is separate from the exchange rate and applicable tax. ATLAS and Amex Travel charge 3.5%, versus roughly 2% on the other cards here. Rewards do not automatically cancel that cost.

Check the actual trip

Compare your lounge list, merchant acceptance, booking price and award availability before applying. Unlimited lounge access is still subject to programme rules and lounge capacity.
