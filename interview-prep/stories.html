01NeoSOFT Technologies

Product Manager, Mumbai, Dec 2025 to present

Lead Engine

Situation. Three teams spent a large share of their time on research before any outreach.

What I did. I built lead generation as an autonomous pipeline and shipped it in 2.5 weeks, with an LLM doing the work that would otherwise need engineering headcount.

Result. Pre-outreach research fell from 41 man-days to about 1 man-hour across three teams, at unchanged throughput and zero running cost.

Use it for: AI and agents, automation, doing more with less, 0 to 1.

Reconciliation platform, zero to pilot in 90 days

Situation. A pre-launch digital general insurer needed banking integration, receipting and reconciliation in place before its own launch.

What I did. As sole product owner, I defined the banking integration, receipting, and two-way and three-way reconciliation flows. I selected the banking partner and the payment gateway.

Result. Zero to pilot in 90 days, delivered ahead of the client's own launch.

Use it for: payments and reconciliation, vendor selection, delivering under a deadline.

Pre-sales on five client pursuits

Situation. Five client pursuits across retail banking, B2B healthcare commerce and lending needed a product view early.

What I did. I ran discovery workshops, designed operational workflows and customer journeys, and wrote the proposals, work breakdown structures and pitch decks.

Result. Five pursuits supported end to end, from discovery to proposal.

Use it for: problem framing, estimation, working with clients and senior stakeholders.

02NPCI Bharat Billpay

Product Manager, Mumbai, Jul 2022 to Nov 2025

NPS on Bharat Connect

Situation. The National Pension System was to become the first investment-cum-savings category on Bharat Connect, under the regulator PFRDA. Contributions needed a 12-digit PRAN that most people do not remember.

What I did. As Category Manager, I coordinated the three central recordkeeping agencies, the trustee bank, the technology service provider and the front-end app partners. I designed a 5-click contribution flow with mobile-number-linked PRAN authentication.

Result. Launched in October 2024, live across a ~15M+ NPS subscriber base.

Use it for: alignment without authority, regulated launches, simplifying a hard user journey.

Banking Connect, India's first interoperable net-banking platform

Situation. RBI initiated an interoperable net-banking platform, built from zero.

What I did. As a core team contributor, I worked on standardised payment flows, settlement and dispute-management processes, and the Lien Marking and Mandate framework for corporate net-banking.

Result. Adopted by top banks and payment aggregators, with interoperability across 10+ partner banks.

Use it for: platforms and standards, settlement and disputes, 0 to 1 in a regulated setting.

Biller Analytics Dashboard

Situation. More than 20,000 billers had no view of their collection performance on BBPS.

What I did. I designed and launched Bharat Connect's first biller-facing analytics surface, covering volume, value, technical and business decline rates, and complaint counts across 30 categories.

Result. ~50K monthly active users at about 30 minutes average session. Report downloads grew from 100 to ~15,000 a month in 12 months (150x).

Use it for: data products, self-serve design, adoption metrics.

Agent Locator and bharat-connect.com

Situation. Consumers could not easily find certified collection agents, and uncertified collectors caused fraud complaints.

What I did. I built bharat-connect.com from scratch and designed the Agent Locator, integrating 8M+ certified physical agents on Google Maps through APIs from operating units such as Spice Money and Fino Payments.

Result. Monthly searches grew from 1,000 to ~200,000 in four months. Fraud complaints from uncertified collectors fell from ~200 a month to near zero. Remote cash-deposit TAT dropped from 4 days to 8 hours.

Use it for: fraud and trust outcomes, partner integrations, growth.

RM performance and client lifecycle model

Situation. Relationship managers across commercial teams were assessed subjectively.

What I did. I designed a model with four weighted parameters: entities onboarded, volume contribution, engagement and process improvement. I reported it to leadership weekly and extended it into client lifecycle management and partner recognition.

Result. Entity onboarding rose, RM engagement became proactive, and client complaints and compliance issues fell. Still in use when I left.

Use it for: KPI frameworks, operating cadence, influencing leadership.

Winner, NBBL Hackathon 2025

Situation. Agent Institution and Operating Unit onboarding took about 90 days, and compliance questions needed manual answers from circulars.

What I did. I built a small language model compliance assistant trained on 2 years of NBBL circulars, and an end-to-end automation for onboarding.

Result. Onboarding TAT fell from 90 days to 15 days, about 83 percent. Won the hackathon.

Use it for: AI, process automation, turning a prototype into proof.

03Kotak Mahindra Bank

Manager, Digital Payments Operations, Mumbai, May 2019 to Jul 2022

Merchant payment reporting redesign

Situation. Daily merchant payment reporting for the acquiring and payment-gateway business was slow and error-prone.

What I did. I automated the daily reporting workflow.

Result. Processing time fell from 9 hours to 1.5 hours. Accuracy rose from 96% to 99.98%, with zero non-technical errors for 12 months in a row.

Use it for: process improvement, automation, data quality.

Chargebacks and representment

Situation. The ecommerce acquiring dispute book needed one owner from start to finish.

What I did. I owned it end to end: issuer chargeback receipt, evidence assembly, representment and settlement adjustment. I also resolved settlement discrepancies across the merchant portfolio.

Result. One accountable owner for disputes and settlement adjustments across the merchant portfolio.

Use it for: cards and disputes, settlement, operational ownership.

Fee dashboards and pricing anomalies

Situation. Fee management in digital payments had little visibility for decision makers.

What I did. In a product-to-operations bridge team, I built Excel and Power BI dashboards on fee management.

Result. Found pricing anomalies worth about INR 1.9 million a year, and surfaced trends used by senior leadership for digital-payments strategy.

Use it for: data-led decisions, revenue leakage, challenging the status quo with data.
