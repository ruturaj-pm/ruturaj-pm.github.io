00Opener

I am a product manager with 8+ years in payments and fintech. The strongest part of my record is three years at NPCI Bharat Billpay on the Bharat Connect rails. I shipped the National Pension System to a 15M+ subscriber base. I contributed to India's first interoperable net-banking platform. I built self-serve analytics for 20,000+ billers.

Before that, I spent three years at Kotak Mahindra Bank in acquiring operations. Chargebacks, representment and the automation of merchant payment reporting.

Today at NeoSOFT I am sole product owner on a banking-integration and two-way and three-way reconciliation platform. Coda's settlement work, its range of payment methods and its Merchant of Record model line up closely with what I have built. That is why this role excites me.
