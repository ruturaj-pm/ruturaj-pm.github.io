Product Manager, Mobile and Digital Apps

00Opener

I'm Ruturaj Sahasrabudhe, a product manager with over eight years of experience, about seven of them in payments, and the last four in Product Manager roles. Today I'm a Product Manager at NeoSOFT Technologies, where I took an insurer's reconciliation platform from zero to pilot in 90 days.

Before that, I spent three years at NPCI Bharat Billpay, which runs India's bill payment network. As Category Manager, I took India's National Pension System live on it. A mobile-number check replaced a 12-digit account number that almost nobody remembers. That gave a five-click flow through our partners' apps, live across about 15 million subscribers. I also designed the Agent Locator on Google Maps. Monthly searches grew from 1,000 to about 200,000 in four months. Fraud complaints about uncertified collectors fell from about 200 a month to near zero.

Earlier, I worked in payments at Kotak Mahindra Bank. At HERE Technologies, I shipped a mobile capture app with engineering.

Why Etihad's app? Etihad is growing fast, toward 38 million passengers a year by 2030. The app is where customers book, check in, add extras and spend miles. Simple journeys and safe payments at scale are what I do best. I'd love to do that for Etihad.

01The company

  • Journey 2030 is the growth plan. Etihad's July 2025 release gives its 2030 goals: more than 125 destinations, more than 170 aircraft and 38 million passengers a year. At the end of 2025 its operating fleet was 127 aircraft. Its network had 110 destinations. Etihad flew to 91 of them in December 2025.
  • Digital is part of the plan. In 2025 Etihad refreshed its brand and launched a new website and mobile app for more self-service. Etihad says these changes, together with its cabin, lounge and ground investments, lifted its overall Net Promoter Score 10 percent year on year.
  • The app is set to grow. Etihad Escapes adds a hotel to a flight booking. It launches on etihad.com in October 2026. Expedia Group B2B supplies the hotel rooms, and Juniper Travel Technology supplies the technology. Later phases bring it into the app, with more personalisation and Etihad Guest integration.

02The role

  • Owns the strategy, roadmap, delivery and performance of Etihad's mobile app portfolio. Reports to the Senior Product Manager, Digital.
  • Runs the full lifecycle of customer-facing mobile experiences: discovery, prioritisation, delivery, launch and optimisation.
  • Works with UX, engineering, architecture and business teams on apps that are intuitive, scalable and fast.
  • Drives Agile delivery: priorities, dependencies, risks and release planning. The JD asks for SAFe and Scrum experience.
  • Decides from customer insight, analytics, market trends and competitor benchmarks, then tracks KPIs, adoption, engagement and feedback.
  • Manages third-party vendors and technology partners for delivery and operational excellence.
  • Speaks for the customer, and balances user needs with business goals and technical limits.

07Domain notes

How the recorded video interview works

  • Etihad's invite asks for about 45 minutes of focused time, a quiet, well-lit space, and a stable device with camera, microphone and internet. Set the device on a steady surface. Dress as you would for an in-person interview.
  • Finish within 48 to 72 hours of the invite. You choose when to do it.
  • Etihad calls the video a chance to bring the application to life beyond the CV and show the person behind the profile. Every candidate goes through the same structured process. Its own tip: be yourself.
  • Log in early and run the system check for camera, microphone and connection. If it passes, you can close the browser and come back later.
  • Use a laptop with the latest Chrome, on home Wi-Fi. Office and university networks can block HireVue. It needs at least 100 kbps, and 350 kbps is recommended.
  • Do the three practice questions first, as often as you like. Nobody else ever sees them. They are generic, so they will not show Etihad's real questions.
  • Before the real interview starts, the screen shows the number and types of questions and an estimated time.
  • Each question appears with a thinking-time countdown. You can start early. A 3-second countdown then starts your answer.
  • An answer timer runs while you speak. Click Done Answering when you finish. When the timer ends, the answer submits by itself.
  • Etihad sets the thinking time, the answer time and the number of attempts per question. HireVue's 2018 guide says 'normally' 30 seconds to prepare and about 3 minutes to answer. The answer time shows on screen with each question, so check it on question one. Keep each answer to its cue card, about 60 to 90 seconds. Add detail only if the timer gives 2 minutes or more.
  • A submitted answer is final. You cannot go back to it. Only Etihad can allow a retake, by sending a new link.
  • If the connection drops between questions, reopen the invite link and carry on at the next question. Earlier answers are saved. If it drops in the middle of an answer, that answer is most likely submitted. Finish the rest, then tell Etihad.
  • Once the due date passes, the link expires. Only Etihad can extend it or send a new invite.
  • Everything the camera sees and the microphone hears is captured. Clear the background, silence the phone and close the door.
  • Notes may sit beside you. HireVue's help centre says looking at the webcam while you speak gives the feel of eye contact.
  • HireVue's FAQ says most video-based assessments have five to eight questions and take under 20 to 30 minutes, and to set aside 45 minutes. Etihad's own question count and estimated time show on screen before you start.
  • Some HireVue interviews add an AI assessment and some do not. Whether Etihad's does is unknown. Speak clearly and give every answer a clear start, middle and end.

One idea for the app

  • The app covers the core trip: book and track flights, check in, save the boarding pass, add extras, get real-time flight alerts, and earn or spend Etihad Guest Miles.
  • Ratings differ by store. In its home market, Apple's App Store shows 4.6 from about 32,000 ratings, No. 23 in Travel. Google Play there shows 3.6 from about 30,500 reviews, with over 1 million downloads. The US App Store shows 4.4 from about 18,000.
  • On the same Google Play page, other airline apps showed Qatar Airways at 4.1, Singapore Airlines at 4.9 and Qantas at 3.8.
  • The iPhone app shipped 25 versions from 22 May to 8 October 2026. Two carry feature notes. Version 8.0.1 (30 July) added Face ID login. Version 8.1.1 (11 September) added a new booking flow, with fare brand comparison, an interactive seat map, a trip summary and payment in multiple currencies. The rest read 'Bug fixes and performance improvements'.
  • Login is the loudest app complaint in the visible reviews. Google Play, February 2026, marked helpful by 48 people: 'you have to log in again and enter OTP codes EVERY SINGLE TIME you open the app or the website', with blank payment screens too. App Store, June 2026: the app asks for login again to add baggage or upgrade.
  • The theme is old. A 2018 Google Play review complained of logging in each time with the 12-digit frequent flyer number plus an emailed code.
  • Payment steps draw complaints too. A December 2023 Google Play review says a miles plus cash price failed at the final payment step.
  • The iPhone listing's accessibility section is empty: 'The developer has not yet indicated which accessibility features this app supports.'
  • Etihad Wallet holds Etihad Guest Miles and Etihad Money for flights and extras, and eligible refunds arrive instantly as Etihad Money. Its page describes it for etihad.com only.

Idea. One sign-in that lasts. Keep customers securely signed in on iPhone and Android with biometric unlock (Face ID or fingerprint). Ask for a one-time password only at payment, or when a sensitive detail changes, such as the email address. Carry the same session into every flow, including seats, bags and upgrades. Step one is to check the data: did the July Face ID release fix this on iPhone, and does Android have the same? It mirrors my pension work at NPCI Bharat Billpay, where a mobile-number check replaced a 12-digit account number and made a five-click flow possible.

How I would prove it. Re-login rate: the share of app sessions where a signed-in customer is asked to log in or enter a one-time password again, split by iPhone and Android. It should fall. To show the value, the completion rate of extras and upgrades started in the app should rise. Safety check: account-takeover and payment-fraud cases must stay flat. Slow signal: the Google Play rating, now 3.6.
