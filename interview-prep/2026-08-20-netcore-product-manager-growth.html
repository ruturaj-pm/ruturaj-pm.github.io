01The company

Netcore Cloud is an AI-powered customer engagement and marketing platform, formerly known as Netcore Smartech. It calls itself "a one-stop growth platform for marketers, growth and product managers". The platform covers journey orchestration, multi-channel messaging (email, SMS, push, WhatsApp), personalisation, A/B testing, user onboarding and product recommendations.

Useful facts

  • Acquired Unbxd, a US search and personalisation company, in 2022, in a deal reported at about $100M. It also bought Boxx.ai, Quinto.ai and Hansel.io.
  • Netcore Unbxd was named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Search and Product Discovery. This is the company's strongest analyst position.
  • Named a Strong Performer in The Forrester Wave for Email Marketing Service Providers, Q1 2026.
  • A heavy marketing push on "Agentic Marketing" in 2026.
  • Competitors: CleverTap, MoEngage, WebEngage, Braze, Insider.

02The role

Product Manager, Growth. The role is scoped at 3 to 6 years.

Which surface it owns is still open: the engagement platform, the Unbxd search and discovery side, or cross-platform activation. That is the first question to ask.

05Likely questions

QuestionMy angle
"The role is scoped at 3 to 6 years. You have 8+.""In three years at NPCI Bharat Billpay I built the analytics and adoption layer for a network of 20,000+ billers. That is the thing Netcore sells. I would like to build it as the core product."
"Walk me through a growth experiment you have run."
  1. Name the boundary first. "I should be straight: I have not run a randomised A/B test with a holdout group. On a national payments network, regulated flows are not split-tested, and rollouts were sequenced partner by partner."
  2. Then show what I did. I defined success metrics before the build, instrumented them, and ran measured before-and-after interventions at scale. Downloads went from 100 to ~15,000 over twelve months. Searches went from 1,000 to ~200,000 in four months. Onboarding TAT fell from 90 days to 15. Complaints fell from ~200 a month to near zero. I iterated off decline-rate and funnel data.
  3. Then show the discipline. Hypothesis and a single primary metric. Minimum detectable effect, and how it sets sample size. Guardrail metrics, so a funnel win does not cost retention. Run duration that covers full weekly cycles. Novelty effects. No peeking at significance mid-flight.
  4. Close it. "So the gap is real and it is narrow. I have the metric discipline. I have not yet owned the split-test mechanics. At a company whose own platform ships A/B testing, that closes in weeks."
"You have no martech background."True. I have not used tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude or CleverTap in a job. What transfers: I have been the customer for this category. Biller Analytics was me building, for 20,000+ billers, the decline-rate and funnel visibility that Netcore sells to marketers. I know what the buyer acts on, because I ran discovery interviews to decide which metrics were worth shipping.

06Questions to ask them

  1. Which surface does this Growth PM own: the engagement platform, the Unbxd search and discovery side, or cross-platform activation?
  2. How does the team run experiments today, and what is the current velocity?
  3. Is the motion self-serve and product-led, or enterprise-assisted? That changes what growth means here.
  4. What does success look like at six months?
  5. How did the Unbxd acquisition change the way product is organised?
