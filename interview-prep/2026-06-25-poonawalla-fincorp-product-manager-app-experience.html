06Questions to ask them

  1. What single metric is this role judged on first in 90 days? Journey completion, approval to disbursal, engagement and retention, or something else?
  2. Where is the biggest drop-off in the lending journey today? Is it seen as friction, trust, or tech and verification?
  3. How mature is the app's instrumentation? Are there clean stage-level funnel events, and can the team A/B test in production? Or is building that early work?
  4. How do product and analytics work day to day? Does the PM self-serve dashboards and define experiments, or partner with a shared analytics pod?
  5. The JD mentions conversational journeys. Is a chat assistant or guided flow already on the roadmap, or is it still at the idea stage?
  6. Why is this role open now? Is it a new mandate to reimagine the app, or a gap on an existing roadmap?

07Domain notes

The likely case: loan journey drop-off

Prompt: "Our loan application journey has high drop-off. How would you diagnose it and improve completion?" A variant: "Make onboarding more conversational."

Treat it as a data-driven product case. Open with: "First I'd look at the funnel data." Speak it in 3 to 4 minutes.

  1. Frame (15 seconds): the north star is funnel completion to disbursal. Map the journey, find the leak, form a hypothesis, test it.
  2. Map the funnel: app open, login and KYC, eligibility and offer, loan details, documents and bank verification, consent and e-sign, submit, approval, disbursal.
  3. Instrument and find the leak: pull stage-to-stage conversion and time on each step. Segment by new and returning users, device and region. Size the single biggest leak first. KYC and document or bank verification are the classic drop points in lending apps.
  4. Find the cause: friction (too many fields), trust (unclear asks, no sense of progress), clarity (jargon, no upfront eligibility) or tech (OCR or verification failures, latency). Link it to "conversational": swap a dense form for a guided, plain-language step.
  5. Prioritise and ship: pick 1 or 2 fixes by impact and effort. Show indicative eligibility upfront. Pre-fill fields. Add a progress bar and save-and-resume. A/B test the changed step. Primary metric: step completion. Guardrail: approval quality and fraud must hold steady.
  6. Measure and iterate: report the stage lift and the downstream effect on disbursal. Roll out. Then attack the next leak. The funnel is a living dashboard.

Link to my work: the Biller Analytics Dashboard (a data product at about 50K MAU) and the Agent Locator (journey and trust, 1,000 to 200,000 searches) both show the same loop. Diagnose with data, ship, measure.
