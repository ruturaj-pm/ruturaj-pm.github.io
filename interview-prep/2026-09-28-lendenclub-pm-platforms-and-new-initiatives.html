LenDenClub: Manager (M2), PM Platforms and New Initiatives

First interview28 September 2026P2P lending platform

The company

  • One of India's largest peer-to-peer (P2P) lending platforms. It is registered with the RBI as an NBFC-P2P, and it is profitable.
  • P2P basics: lenders and borrowers meet on the platform. Money moves through escrow accounts run by a bank-promoted trustee. It never passes through the platform's own books.
  • The RBI tightened P2P rules in August 2024: no assured returns, no guarantees or credit enhancement, no "instant liquidity" products, and faster movement of escrow funds.
  • What the role covers: one platform shared by several business units, agentic AI to automate internal work (ops, KYC, underwriting support, vendor workflows), and ownership of the payment and vendor partners.

My 60-second opener

"I'm a payments product manager with 8+ years across a bank, a national payment rail and a technology services firm.

At Kotak I ran acquiring operations: chargebacks, representment and merchant reporting. At NPCI Bharat Billpay I spent three years on the rail itself. I took the National Pension System live as a new category, working with the three recordkeeping agencies, the trustee bank and the app partners under PFRDA. I contributed to Banking Connect, India's first interoperable net-banking platform. I also built the analytics dashboard that 20,000+ billers use.

Today at NeoSOFT I'm product owner on a reconciliation platform for a digital insurer, where I chose the banking partner and the payment gateway. I also built an agentic pipeline that cut pre-outreach research from 41 man-days to about one man-hour.

Payments plumbing, partner management and AI workflow automation is the mix this platform role asks for. That is why I'm excited about it."

Six stories, ready to tell

1. Lead Engine (agentic automation, efficiency)

Three teams spent 41 man-days on research before any outreach. I built lead generation as an autonomous pipeline in 2.5 weeks, with an LLM doing the work engineers would have done. Result: about 1 man-hour, same throughput, zero running cost. Link to this role: "I would apply the same approach to KYC checks or vendor onboarding here."

2. NPS on Bharat Connect (alignment without authority)

The first investment-cum-savings category on the platform, launched October 2024. I coordinated the three central recordkeeping agencies, the trustee bank, the technology service provider and the front-end app partners, under PFRDA. Mobile-number-linked PRAN authentication replaced 12-digit PRAN memorisation, in a 5-click contribution flow. Live across a ~15M+ subscriber base.

3. Reconciliation platform, zero to pilot in 90 days (vendors, payments)

Sole product owner for a pre-launch digital general insurer: banking integration, receipting, and two-way and three-way reconciliation. I chose the banking partner and the payment gateway. Delivered ahead of the client's own launch.

4. Biller Analytics Dashboard (a platform used by many)

20,000+ billers across 30 categories had no view of their collection performance. I designed a self-serve surface covering volume, value, technical and business declines, and complaints. ~50K monthly active users at a ~30-minute average session length. Report downloads grew from 100 in month 1 to ~15,000 by month 12.

5. Agent Locator (fraud and ops outcome)

8M+ certified agents on Google Maps via API. Monthly searches went from 1,000 to ~200,000 in four months. Fraud complaints from uncertified collectors fell from ~200 a month to near zero. Remote cash-deposit TAT dropped from 4 days to 8 hours.

6. Onboarding automation, NBBL Hackathon 2025 (process framework)

End-to-end automation of Agent Institution and Operating Unit onboarding cut TAT from 90 to 15 days, about 83 percent. The same winning entry included a small language model compliance assistant trained on 2 years of circulars.

Likely questions, and my angle

QuestionAngle
How would you design an agentic workflow for KYC or loan ops?Map the process step by step. Automate the steps with clear inputs and outputs first. Keep a human in the loop on decisions that touch credit or compliance. Add guardrails and a full audit trail, because RBI and DPDP will ask. Measure TAT, error rate and manual touches before and after. Start with one workflow, then turn it into a reusable playbook.
Two business PMs want conflicting things from the platform. What do you do?Get both requirements onto one page. Find the shared core and build it once. Make the trade-off visible against the quarterly KPIs, and escalate with data only if needed. NPS is my example: many partners, none reporting to me.
Tell me about a vendor or partner who missed an SLA.Use the reconciliation platform or the NPCI operating-unit work. Cover how it was measured, the escalation path, the commercial lever, and what changed in the contract or integration so it did not repeat.
Walk me through reconciliation for a lending or payments flow.Three-way match: bank statement, gateway or escrow settlement file, and the internal ledger. Handle exceptions with ageing buckets. Watch for T+1 timing gaps, reversals and chargebacks. Automate the matching and staff the exceptions.
How do UPI rails fit into lending?UPI AutoPay mandates and e-NACH for EMI collection, UPI for disbursal, failure and retry handling, and daily settlement reconciliation.
How do you prioritise with man-day estimates?Impact on the KPI divided by effort in man-days, with confidence as a check. Cascade company KPIs to business-unit KPIs to initiatives. Revisit every quarter.
