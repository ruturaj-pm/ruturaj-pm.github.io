01The company

  • One of India's largest peer-to-peer (P2P) lending platforms. It is registered with the RBI as an NBFC-P2P, and it is profitable.
  • P2P basics: lenders and borrowers meet on the platform. Money moves through escrow accounts run by a bank-promoted trustee. It never passes through the platform's own books.
  • The RBI tightened P2P rules in August 2024: no assured returns, no guarantees or credit enhancement, no "instant liquidity" products, and faster movement of escrow funds.

02The role

The role covers three things.

  • One platform shared by several business units.
  • Agentic AI to automate internal work: ops, KYC, underwriting support and vendor workflows.
  • Ownership of the payment and vendor partners.

05Likely questions

QuestionMy angle
How would you design an agentic workflow for KYC or loan ops?Map the process step by step. Automate the steps with clear inputs and outputs first. Keep a human in the loop on decisions that touch credit or compliance. Add guardrails and a full audit trail, because RBI and DPDP will ask. Measure TAT, error rate and manual touches before and after. Start with one workflow, then turn it into a reusable playbook.
Two business PMs want conflicting things from the platform. What do you do?Get both requirements onto one page. Find the shared core and build it once. Make the trade-off visible against the quarterly KPIs, and escalate with data only if needed. NPS is my example: many partners, none reporting to me.
Tell me about a vendor or partner who missed an SLA.Use the reconciliation platform or the NPCI operating-unit work. Cover how it was measured, the escalation path, the commercial lever, and what changed in the contract or integration so it did not repeat.
Walk me through reconciliation for a lending or payments flow.Three-way match: bank statement, gateway or escrow settlement file, and the internal ledger. Handle exceptions with ageing buckets. Watch for T+1 timing gaps, reversals and chargebacks. Automate the matching and staff the exceptions.
How do UPI rails fit into lending?UPI AutoPay mandates and e-NACH for EMI collection, UPI for disbursal, failure and retry handling, and daily settlement reconciliation.
How do you prioritise with man-day estimates?Impact on the KPI divided by effort in man-days, with confidence as a check. Cascade company KPIs to business-unit KPIs to initiatives. Revisit every quarter.
Why LenDenClub? Why this role?A profitable, regulated fintech where the platform, payments and AI automation sit in one role. That matches my last three jobs combined. Lending is the next rail I want to own.
